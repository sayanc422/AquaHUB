## What "unverified" means here

These were supplied for design work and are in the repository so the site can be built and reviewed
against real photographs instead of grey boxes. None of them has a licence on record.

Before the shop takes a single order, every row above needs to become one of:

* **own** — photographed by the shop. Best answer: it also shows the actual fish for sale, which is
  what a customer is buying.
* **licensed** — a stock licence covering commercial use, with the licence number in this table.
* **breeder** — supplied by the breeder or importer with written permission, filed.

Aquarium photography is routinely watermarked and routinely enforced, and a shop is a commercial use
with no fair-dealing argument available.

## Processing applied

Originals were 5.6 MB across ten files. Each was resized to 1600 px wide at most, re-encoded as
progressive JPEG at quality 82 (76 for `auratus.jpg`, whose rock background compresses badly), and
**stripped of EXIF** — camera metadata can carry GPS coordinates and an owner's name, which a shop
has no reason to publish. Total is now 1.8 MB.

Two are below the 1200 px minimum in `README.md` and should be re-shot:

* `nkhomo-benga-peacock.jpg` — 1136 px wide. Acceptable on a card, soft as a hero.
* `salvini.jpg` — **474 px wide.** Too small for anything but a thumbnail.
